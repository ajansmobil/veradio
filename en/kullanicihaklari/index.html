User Rights

Everyone has the right to request the protection of their personal data. The protection of your personal data is very important to us, and in this regard, we act within the framework of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and related legislation. Users have the right to be informed about the purposes for which their data is collected, how it is stored, and with whom it is shared.

You can submit requests to update, correct, delete, and anonymize your personal data. You can also request detailed information about the purpose for which the collected data is used and the processing process, and obtain information about third parties involved in these processes. You can always contact us to exercise your rights.

You can find detailed information about the protection of your personal data and your rights by reviewing our Privacy Policy on our website. As a user, the security of your data is our priority, and we have taken the necessary technical and administrative measures.
